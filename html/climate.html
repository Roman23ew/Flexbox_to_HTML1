What does a reportes saying about the climate change?

Georgia has a ranking of 104th in the World Risk Report for2023 with medium exposure, vulnerability and has a moderate lack of adaptive capacities. Georgia is expected to face an increase in average temperatures and droughts by 2080. Regional disparities in living standards are expected to increase due to climate change, though it is possible that the relatively poorer communities in mountainous areas may be able to grow more diverse crops due to increases in temperatures.
